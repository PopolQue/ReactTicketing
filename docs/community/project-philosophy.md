# Project Philosophy

ReactTicket was built to solve the fragmentation of event ticketing solutions. Often, organizers have to rely on expensive 3rd-party services that hijack the customer journey and own the data.

Our goal is to provide a fully self-hosted, modular ticketing engine that fits _inside_ your product.

## Non-Goals

- **We are not a payment processor.** We provide the hooks to integrate Stripe, PayPal, or crypto gateways, but we do not handle money.
- **We are not a CMS.** We handle event _ticketing_, not event _marketing_. You build the landing page; we handle the cart and the barcode.
